# git-dragon

An evolving, animated pixel-art dragon for GitHub profile READMEs.

- It **evolves** through six stages as your contribution count grows.
- It is **tinted** by your most-used languages (GitHub Linguist colours).
- It is **animated**: wing flaps, blinking, fire breath (adolescent and up), embers and stars (legendary).
- It is **deterministic**: identical input gives byte-identical output.
